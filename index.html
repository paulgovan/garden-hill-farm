Our Story

About the farm

Garden Hill Farm is a family-owned farm in Lyman, Spartanburg County. We keep horses here on open pasture, with a red barn, and the family home on the property.

The land was once a peach farm, part of the Upstate's long history of peach growing. Most of it is pasture now, though we still grow peaches on the property.

The farm is also home base for Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services, Dr. Kendrick Govan's mobile practice serving horse owners in Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina.

Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services

Mobile equine veterinary care

Magnolia Equine is a mobile veterinary practice based at Garden Hill Farm. Instead of hauling horses to a clinic, Dr. Govan sees them at their home barns across Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina.

KG

Kendrick Govan, DVM, DACT

Dr. Govan is a Diplomate of the American College of Theriogenologists (DACT), which means board certification in theriogenology: the veterinary specialty covering reproduction, from breeding through pregnancy and foaling.

Reproduction

Breeding and reproductive care from a board-certified theriogenologist.

Equine Dentistry

Routine dental exams and care, done at the horse's home barn.

Wellness Services

Preventive and routine care to keep horses on a regular health schedule.
