Our Story

A family farm in the Upstate

Garden Hill Farm is our family farm: a place to raise horses, look after the land, and enjoy a slower, country pace of life. The land was once a peach farm, and today it's pasture and barns.

It's also home base for Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services, which cares for horses all across the region.

Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services

Mobile horse care, based right here

Magnolia Equine is a mobile veterinary practice that provides onsite care for the equine community across Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina.

KG

Kendrick Govan, DVM, DACT

Dr. Govan is a Diplomate of the American College of Theriogenologists (DACT), the board certification for veterinarians specializing in animal reproduction.

Reproduction

Breeding and reproductive care for horses, backed by board certification in theriogenology.
