The Farm

Rooted in the Upstate

Set in the rolling countryside of Lyman, South Carolina, Garden Hill Farm is a place built around horses: open pasture, quiet barns, and the everyday care that keeps them healthy and content.

The farm is also home base for Manolia Equine Veterinary Services, so the same commitment to good horsemanship you see in our fields carries through to the care we offer horses and owners across the region.

Manolia Equine Veterinary Services

Caring for Horses Across the Upstate

Based at Garden Hill Farm, Manolia Equine Veterinary Services provides veterinary care for horses in Lyman and the surrounding area.

Wellness & Preventive Care

Annual exams, vaccinations, Coggins testing, and parasite control programs.

Dentistry

Routine dental exams and floating to keep horses comfortable and eating well.

Lameness Evaluation

Thorough exams to find the source of soundness problems and plan treatment.

Sick Horse Care

Diagnosis and treatment for colic, injuries, illness, and other concerns.

Pre-Purchase Exams

Careful evaluations to help buyers make informed decisions.

Health Certificates

Paperwork for travel, shows, and sales.
