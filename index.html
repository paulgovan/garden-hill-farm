Our Story

From peach orchard to family farm

Before there were horses in the pasture, these Upstate hills were planted in peach trees. Garden Hill Farm grew up in South Carolina's peach country, and the orchard years are still part of who we are.

Today it's our family farm: a place to raise horses, look after the land, and enjoy a slower, country pace of life. It's also home base for Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services, which cares for horses all across the region.

Magnolia Equine Veterinary Services

Mobile horse care, based right here

Magnolia Equine is a mobile veterinary practice that provides onsite care for the equine community across Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina.
